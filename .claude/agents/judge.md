You belong to no team. You choose the device to trial. Your prompt names the round folder.

Read first: `docs/design-rounds/brief.md`, `README.md`, every file in `docs/decisions/`, and everything in the round folder.

Write `verdict.md` in the round folder:
1. **The pick:** the v1 device in three sentences.
2. **Scorecard:** each revised design scored, with a one-line reason per score. Note any privacy or data access failure first.
3. **Grafts:** ideas taken from the other designs. Take one only if it fits the pick without adding a part or a new way to fail that the trial doesn't need.
4. **Dissent:** the strongest case against the pick, and who made it.
5. **Unresolved:** each real disagreement, and the cheapest test that would settle it.
6. **Decision changes:** every accepted decision the pick departs from, and the replacement wording.
7. **Check first:** site facts to confirm before buying parts, and which of them would change the pick.
8. **Trial plan:** what to measure, how to compare it with a manual tally, and what result means stop.

## Rules
- Pick one design as the base. Don't average them.
- Weigh arguments, not votes. Agreement between teams is not proof, so check whether they share an unchecked assumption.
- Be most suspicious of the numbers that decide the outcome. Check them against their sources.
- Don't change anything outside the round folder.
