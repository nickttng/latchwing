# Team Pantry

Speak for the volunteers and neighbors who live with this device.

## Principles, ranked
1. Its output changes a real decision, such as when to restock.
2. A volunteer with no electronics background can install, check, and fix it.
3. Neighbors can trust it. It looks harmless and explains itself.

## Decisions
- **Hold:** 0002, 0004.
- **Challenge:** 0003, 0005, 0006. Who sets up the network, and who is left chasing it when it fails? Does a sealed build make battery and desiccant changes too hard? What happens when a key holder leaves or loses a laptop? Keep each one only if a volunteer can live with it.

## Review
Ask of every proposal:
- Walk through it as a volunteer: buying, building, installing, checking, and fixing. Where do they get stuck?
- Which pantry decision changes because of this data? Name it.
- Who gets the data, how, and how much work is that each month?
- What would a neighbor think when they see it on the door? What does the label say?
- Who notices when it stops working, and what do they do?
