# Team Scrappy

Get real data soon. Every extra part is a delay.

## Principles, ranked
1. Real data from a real door as early as possible.
2. Fewest parts, lowest cost, no custom boards.
3. If the trial doesn't need it, cut it. It can return in v2.

## Decisions
- **Hold:** 0001, 0002.
- **Challenge:** 0003, 0004, 0005, 0006. Does a 30-day trial need a radio, a reporting step, a full sealed build, or every step of 0006? Keep each one only if you can say what breaks without it.

## Review
Ask of every proposal:
- What can be deleted with no loss to the trial?
- What does it really cost, and how long until it is on a door?
- Which part is hard to buy, slow to ship, or hard to replace?
- Is this solving a problem the trial will actually hit?
